# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback and focus on ideas rather than individuals.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers learn.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Keep discussions relevant and professional.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam, or other conduct that makes participation hostile.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Please include relevant context or links where possible. Reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled as confidentially as practical.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and other interactions where someone is representing or participating in this project.
